About Valley Crest Capital

Who we are

More than a financial adviser

Valley Crest Capital provides businesses, public institutions and development organisations with financial and operational services held to a high professional standard.

We offer project management, financial services, procurement and ethical mineral mandate advisory. Our work rests on careful analysis, transparent reporting and a firm grasp of the Kenyan and East African business environment.

Our office is at iHub Plaza on James Gichuru Road, Lavington, Nairobi.
